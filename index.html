PDF Send

Share Over Wi-Fi

Move PDFs between your phone and your PC

PDF Send puts a small web server on your phone. Your computer opens the address it shows — in any browser — and the file transfers straight across your own Wi-Fi. Nothing is uploaded to a server, because there isn't one.

  • No cables, no cloud, no accounts Your files go device to device over your own network.
  • Works both ways Send PDFs to your PC, or send them from your PC back to your phone.
  • Scan or type Scan the QR code, or type the short address shown on screen.
  • Optional PIN Require a code before anything can be downloaded or uploaded.
  • Offline hotspot No shared Wi-Fi? The phone can host its own network for the transfer.
